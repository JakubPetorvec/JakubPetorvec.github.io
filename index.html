The modules

Four modules, one account.

Every module has its own colour and its own llama, so you always know where you are.

Home

Split the chores, tick them off, and run a bigger cleanup together.

Shopping

One list of what the household still needs, open to everyone in the space.

Plans

Collect ideas, settle on a date, and get ready for it together.

Vault

Follow values and goals you type in yourself. Vault does not move money and is not a bank, a lender or an investment service.

How it works

Your space, shared on your terms.

  1. Solo or shared

    Use Lama on your own, or open a space with the people you live with.

  2. Pick a module

    Home, Shopping, Plans and Vault each stay focused on one job.

  3. Add what matters

    Chores, items, plans, values. Everything you enter stays in your space.

  4. Stay in sync

    Everyone with access to a module sees the same list you do.

Where things stand

Lama is being built. It is not in the app stores yet and there is no launch date to share. This page describes the modules as they are designed, and screens can still change before release.

Questions are welcome in the meantime. Support and contact.

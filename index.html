One calm little llama for everyday life.

Lama gathers home chores, shopping lists, shared plans and a personal values vault into a single warm, uncluttered space — for you, or for a household sharing it together.

Explore the modules How it works

Lama is still pre-release. There is no app-store link or public launch yet.

Four modules, one space

Everything lives in its own pastel corner.

Each module keeps its own colour and its own llama, so you always know where you are.

Home

Split chores, mark them done, and run a shared cleanup together.

Shopping

Keep a running list of what your household still needs to buy.

Plans

Collect ideas, agree on dates, and get ready for things together.

Vault

Track your own values and goals over time, entered by you. Vault does not move money and is not a bank, lender or investment service.

How it works

Your space, shared on your terms.

Solo or shared

Use Lama alone, or share a space with the people you live with.

Pick a module

Open Home, Shopping, Plans or Vault — each one stays focused on its own job.

Add what matters

Chores, list items, plans or values — everything you enter stays in your space.

Stay in sync

Share updates with the people who have access to each module.

Where things stand

Lama is being built and is not yet available in app stores. This page describes the modules as designed; features and screens may still change before release.

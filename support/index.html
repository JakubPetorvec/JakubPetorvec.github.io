Support

Ask a person.

Lama is built by one person, so questions, bug reports and requests about your account all land in the same inbox.

Write in

Email is the only channel for now. There is no ticket form and no chat widget.

jakubpetrovec321@gmail.com

It helps to say which module you were in, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot usually says the rest.

Never send your password. Nobody working on Lama will ask you for it.

Your account and your data

You can ask what your account holds, ask for a copy of it, ask to have something corrected, or ask for the account to be closed.

  • Write from the address you signed up with, so the request can be matched to the right account.
  • Say plainly what you want done.

Nothing here is automated. Every request is read and handled by hand while Lama is in development, so a reply can take a while.
